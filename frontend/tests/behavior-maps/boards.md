# Boards behavior map

Baseline: commit 7ca93d32; owners updated for the F2 consolidation on base 1be762d2. Loop titles are quoted as source templates, with `${…}` left unexpanded. Paths are under `frontend/tests`; `:N` is the line of the `test(` call. Source `—` means no criterion, contract, or bug is evident in the test or its comments. `D4` marks a protected test (consolidation.md D4): a 401/403/404 denial, reader or guest access, data isolation, or a regression-shaped race (the comment or title describes a stale or late response; no commit evidence was checked, so treat these as protected until history says otherwise).

Layer column: `http` runs a browser (`page` or `browser` fixture); `isolated` runs in Node without a browser. The 109 tests that lived in `http/boards-*` without a `page` fixture (static markup through `renderToStaticMarkup`, reducers, stubbed `globalThis.fetch`) moved unchanged to `isolated/boards/live-*.spec.ts`, except two same-rule merges into the tables of `isolated/boards/board-scope-rules.spec.ts`. Other `isolated` rows appear where an http test of this family repeats the rule.
